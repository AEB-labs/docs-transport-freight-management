# Acceptance Process

At the beginning of the implementation, AEB will provide you with a questionnaire that will ask for all the information required to connect to the carrier. You will then use this information to configure the carrier in Carrier Connect.

Once the carrier is fully configured, you can begin the acceptance process. To do this, you will create the required test shipments and generate labels and EDI, which will then be reviewed by the carrier. You will receive the exact acceptance requirements from your carrier contact.

Once you receive acceptance from the carrier, we will transfer the carrier configuration to the production system so you can use it productively.
