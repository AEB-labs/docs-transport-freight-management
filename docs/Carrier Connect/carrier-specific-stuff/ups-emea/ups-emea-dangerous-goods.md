Please check available hazardous types in our [Functional scope of the carriers offered](https://rz3.aeb.de/docudata/system-descriptions/carrier-connect/sd-carrierconnect-carrieroverview/en-US/index.html#index_content)

For fully regulated hazardous goods you need to have webservice credentials filled out in the account setup in the [carrier configuration](https://dash.readme.com/project/transport-freight-management/v3/docs/carrier-configuration). UPS is thus notified in advance of the dangerous goods to be transported in the shipping orders. In case you only transport Limited Quantity (LQ), no webservice credentials are needed. 

The UPS Webservice also includes a data check from UPS side with regard to your transmitted hazardous goods data. Hence, you need to use correct data, such as correct hazardous goods material description in order to get your request validated. See /search for updated "UPS Dangerous Goods Chemical Tables" online.
