Thank you for your interest in our Carrier Select API. We would like to make it as easy as possible for you to get started using Carrier Select. Please feel free to contact us if you miss information or if you find a way to make things even easier.

Carrier Select is used to calculate proposals for suitable carriers, services, and value-added services for (planned) shipments. The results can also be multi-level. For each proposal, additional information can be provided, e.g. validation, freight costs, transit times, freely definable ratings depending on the setup. Some of this information is retrieved from other systems: validation from Carrier Connect, freight costs from Logistics Cost Management, and lead times from Monitoring\&Alerting.
