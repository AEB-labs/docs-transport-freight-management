Document service allows you to create, store, upload and print documents and labels.  
As the general principle of document creation, a template provided by the document service is combined with data provided by the calling system. Templates generating a printable PDF as well as a label can be used.  
In addition, external documents can be uploaded, printed, and stored (short-term) or even archived (long-term).
