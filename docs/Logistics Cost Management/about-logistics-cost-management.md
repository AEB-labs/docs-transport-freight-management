Logistics Cost Management enables the automated price calculation and invoicing of services. It can be used by service providers who want to invoice. Or it can be used by ordering parties to check the prices of service providers. The most important use case for this is freight cost management. 
With Logistics Cost Management, prices for services can be calculated automatically and compiled into invoices. The invoices are transferred to the accounting system for posting.
