## Swabian Clothes

In this example we are going to look at a customer *Swabian Clothes*.\
A clothing manufacturer, that delivers his clothes to department stores around the country.

*Swabian Clothes* currently uses an ERP tool to manage their orders, however they want more insight into their supply chain and give their customers more insight for their orders, which is why they chose to connect their ERP to the *Monitoring & Alerting* by AEB.
